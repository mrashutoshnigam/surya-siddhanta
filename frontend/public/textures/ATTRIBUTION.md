# Planet textures

Planet maps by Solar System Scope / INOVE, downloaded without alteration from
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ on 2026-11-06.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Files are the published 2k maps: sun, mercury, venus_atmosphere, earth_daymap,
moon, mars, jupiter, saturn, uranus, and neptune. Names omit the original `2k_`
prefix. No modifications have been made. Maps use enhanced colors and may
include reconstructed terrain; they are appearance illustrations, not live images.

Source imagery includes NASA missions. Attribution is also displayed in the
Universe Explorer. A deterministic procedural texture is used when a map fails
to load.
